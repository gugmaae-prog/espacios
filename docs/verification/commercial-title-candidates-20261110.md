# Commercial map title candidates — 10 November 2026

The existing owner-authorized [9 November RGEstate full index](../../data/uae-evidence/rgestate/full-index-validation.json) contains 1,670 publisher listing IDs. The commercial category included 863 sale and rent IDs. This release derives [137 additional title candidates](../../data/uae-evidence/rgestate/commercial-title-candidates-validation.json) from the same checksum-verified index, without a fresh scrape or photos. The reproducible classifier selects sale/rent paths with explicit built-space title terms and excludes plot, land, yard, residential, project, and the observed `plor` typo. The derived source is tied to the full-index raw SHA-256 and has its own compressed and decoded checksums.

The combined `/map/api/uae-evidence/commercial/listings` and `/areas` views contain 1,000 listing IDs: 863 publisher commercial-category rows and 137 title matches. The `scope` query (`all`, `category`, `title-candidates`) preserves this distinction. The UI labels each card and offers the same source filter. The 137 additions include 107 rent and 30 sale paths. They add 86 rows to reviewed area-reference circles and leave 51 without one; all 51 remain in the unfiltered list. The combined area map has 894 area-referenced rows and 106 unresolved rows. Area circles are not unit pins.

The title matches are candidates, not publisher-confirmed commercial-category members. Some IDs may refer to the same physical space. Asking price, use type, and current availability need advertiser confirmation; no property photo, registered sale, or rent amount was inferred. The separate Ajman, Sharjah, RAK, and Fujairah historical evidence cards retain their original meaning and do not enter listing counts. The accepted V48 project and history totals are unchanged.
