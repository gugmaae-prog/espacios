# UAE commercial-space evidence audit — 3 November 2026

## Published historical evidence

- [Ajman Land and Real Estate Regulation's Real Estate Units Sales](https://data.ajman.ae/explore/dataset/real-estate-units-sales/) is published under CC BY 4.0. The existing 21 August 2026 snapshot has 6,075 quarterly aggregate rows. The Office and Shop subset contains **89 aggregate rows** across **22 source project labels**: Office 64 rows / 315 reported sales / AED 103,442,285, Shop 25 rows / 31 reported sales / AED 17,886,872. The publisher's live grouped API returned those same subtype totals on 3 November. The source's retained raw SHA-256 is `801cafef1f269d919ace2282f4f5cec8e7a021ce0241eba040f904a9fd672afd`.
- Map v49 serves that exact subset at `/map/api/uae-evidence/ajman/commercial-sales`, backed by the checksum-verified private IDrive E2 source object. It supports Office/Shop, year, quarter, exact project label, and pagination filters. The 346 sum is a **reported sale count across aggregate rows**, not 346 uniquely identifiable or currently available spaces. No floor area, unit identifier, current ask, rent, coordinate or V48 canonical project link is established.
- [Ajman Municipality's Certified Rent Contracts](https://data.ajman.ae/explore/dataset/certified-rent-contracts-in-ajman/) and its separate [Manama](https://data.ajman.ae/explore/dataset/certified-rent-contract-in-manama/) and [Masfoot](https://data.ajman.ae/explore/dataset/certified-rent-contracts-in-masfoot/) datasets are also CC BY 4.0. The existing `/map/api/uae-evidence/ajman/rent-contracts?city=ajman&category=Commercial` route returns 213 commercial-category source rows; Manama has 213 and Masfoot has 204. These are **certified contract-count rows**, not rent amounts, available units or a reliable cross-city total. Source vintages and missing-value coverage differ.

## Active sale and lease inventory

The Map v50 commercial page uses a **publisher-listed snapshot**, not independently confirmed live availability. The user confirmed Espacios may reuse factual details and links from all RGEstate listings, including third-party advertisers, and asked to omit photos. The snapshot was captured from [RGEstate's commercial rent category](https://www.rgestate.com/property-for-rent/commercial) and [commercial sale category](https://www.rgestate.com/property-for-sale/commercial) on 3 November 2026. It has **894 unique source listing IDs**: 477 rent and 417 sale, across 43 category pages. Two independent full-index reads matched exactly; every category row matched those full-index facts. Each captured category page was checked against its recorded SHA-256. The normalized gzip and decoded JSON are checked again at build and Worker read time.

`/map/commercial` provides search, sale/rent filters, direct source links, and a photo-free card design. `/map/api/uae-evidence/commercial/listings` is the read-only, paginated source API. The immutable snapshot preserves the source listing ID, title, location label when supplied, square-foot area only when explicitly present in the card's area field, price only when the source amount and period are interpretable, purpose and link. **124 rows lack a source area field, 15 lack a location label, and uncertain or zero price labels remain null.** It does not assign coordinates, a V48 project ID, a registered transaction, or an availability verification. Source pages may change after capture. These RGEstate records are **asking listings** and cannot be added to the Ajman registered-sales totals.

- The public PSR Data Hub currently exposes 22 commercial-tagged **project cards**: 2 tagged `Offices` and 20 tagged `Commercial property`. Its registry snapshot is dated 27 September 2026 and responses carry `x-espacios-data-degraded: true`. Some cards mix residential and commercial types, and their project-level prices cannot be assigned to a particular commercial unit. They are discovery leads, **not a verified active sale/lease feed**.
- The current `cba-property-db` has zero rows in `hg_agent_property_finder_listings` and zero in `hg_agent_secondary_units` at this audit. The associated Property Finder profile importer is bounded to linked advisor profiles, but no imported unit can be treated as active when the table is empty. No available commercial unit was published from those tables.
- [Dubai Land Department's Trakheesi API registration](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/api-gateway-registration?appId=3) advertises listing validation and delisting services, but requires a subscription and its posted API terms describe internal business use. No entitlement or reusable public listing feed was found in the Map or PSR Worker bindings. Scraped marketplace listings are not a substitute for a publisher or owner feed with redistribution rights and a timestamped availability check.

Independently confirmed active inventory remains **unavailable**; the RGEstate publisher snapshot and historical Ajman evidence are separately labelled. A future availability-verified record needs a fresh provider confirmation, observed time, expiry/removal behavior, and source link. No all-UAE completeness percentage is asserted.
