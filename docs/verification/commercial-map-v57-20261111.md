## Registered sales map

A separate map mode uses the already published [Dubai registered commercial unit sales source](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dubairealestatedata/dubai-real-estate-sales-transactions), a derived DLD Office/Shop sale subset whose source link and CC BY 4.0 attribution travel with the API. It selects the complete **2025** source year: **6,092** sale records. Four exact reviewed DLD area names have corresponding OpenStreetMap area reference points: Business Bay (1,167), Al Jadaf (112), Al Wasl (1), and Mirdif (8). Their **1,288** sale records can be represented at these area references; the other **4,804** records remain in the source area and record APIs without a map point. The area references are not cadastral centroids, buildings, or individual unit locations. Counts include source-quality-flagged records; monetary summaries and medians use the source's clean-row rule. This map does not mix registered sales with the asking-listing layer or infer current availability.

The [2026 commercial-unit source slice](https://espacios.me/map/api/uae-evidence/dubai/commercial-unit-sales-2026-partial) ends 9 February and remains explicitly partial and separate. V48's 1,645 projects, 1,860 record-history entries, and 16,872 native series are unaffected. R2 remains disabled; the Worker uses verified IDrive E2 for private evidence and checksum-verified embedded commercial snapshots.
