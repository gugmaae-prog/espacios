February, March, June, and September remain missing. The data file and each source article body were read twice and SHA-256 checked; the validation manifest pins the retained numerical facts and links. No article text or images are republished.

All-procedure totals include activity beyond sales; the rounded all-use values are not commercial-only. The five months must not be summed as a January–September series.
