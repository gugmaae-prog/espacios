The 1,158 project/source points are retained as reported, not all independently surveyed project sites. The 360 community-centre points indicate approximate areas and must not be used as project-site locations. Together these 1,518 points are renderable on the map; the other 127 records remain searchable.

The read-only `/map/api/uae-evidence/coverage` endpoint reports this accepted V48 population by emirate alongside 25 separately published authority or reference source snapshots and their API routes, row grain, dates and rights labels. It draws its counts from the checksum-verified accepted heatmap and its source metadata from the committed validation reports. It leaves the authoritative all-UAE project denominator and completeness percentage null. Source rows cannot be summed across classes, and listing an API route does not assert that its backing storage is healthy on every request. Fujairah has selected municipal permit counts, Ras Al Khaimah has selected annual market values, and Umm Al Quwain has a dated 2021 industrial land-lease guide. None of those sources establishes a project register or current unit inventory; their V48 records remain visible.

The V48 record archive has 1,860 project and community records and 16,872 native history series. Its 2,717 approved recovery objects are verified in private IDrive E2 with immutable fallback. This archive does not establish complete current UAE market coverage or recover the original history and saved model bundles.

The [Umm Al Quwain industrial guide audit](uaq-industrial-guide-20261110.md) adds four selected numerical plot-category facts from the authority's 2021 investor guide. The PDF table does not state the AED/sqm rate period, and the guide is not evidence of current availability or built commercial units. This source is kept outside map markers, observed sales/rents, asking listings and forecasts.

The [3 November commercial-space audit](commercial-spaces-20261103.md) records Map v49's Ajman Office and Shop sale aggregates and the separate commercial certified-rent counts. It also records that no verified active sale or lease unit feed is connected; commercial-tagged PSR project cards are not live unit inventory. Map v48's separately verified Abu Dhabi source polygons are documented there as well. Neither release changes the V48 project or map-pin counts.

The [RGEstate full-index audit](rgestate-full-index-20261103.md) validates 1,708 publisher-listed facts and exposes them through a separate backend API. Its 894 verified commercial-category rows overlap the existing commercial API; the other 814 are not automatically residential. A strict, source-label-only derivation identifies 604 listings with an explicit emirate label and leaves 1,104 unassigned; the coverage API reports those asking-listing counts separately for all seven emirates. This cross-emirate publisher index is not included in the per-emirate official-source inventory below.
