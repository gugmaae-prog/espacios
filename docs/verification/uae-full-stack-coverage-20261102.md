The 1,158 project/source points are retained as reported, not all independently surveyed project sites. The 360 community-centre points indicate approximate areas and must not be used as project-site locations. Together these 1,518 points are renderable on the map; the other 127 records remain searchable.

The V48 record archive has 1,860 project and community records and 16,872 native history series. Its 2,717 approved recovery objects are verified in private IDrive E2 with immutable fallback. This archive does not establish complete current UAE market coverage or recover the original history and saved model bundles.

## Additional source-backed backend data

- [SCAD Abu Dhabi Census 2024 real estate table](https://census.scad.gov.ae/home/realestate?lang=en): 8 region-year stock rows for 2023 and 2024, recording rounded units and buildings for the emirate total and the Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, and Al Dhafra regions. The exact public source response is retained with SHA-256 validation; the read-only API is `/map/api/uae-evidence/abu-dhabi/stock`. SCAD's [terms](https://www.scad.gov.ae/web/guest/terms-of-use) permit reproduction with full attribution and its [open-data policy](https://scad.gov.ae/documents/d/guest/scad-open-data-policy) permits sharing open data with source credit. The figures include non-residential uses, are rounded to multiples of five, and use revised 2023 data. They are not project identities, registered sales, map coordinates, or an all-UAE stock census.
- [AD-SDI Urban Development Projects](https://arcgis.sdi.abudhabi.ae/agspublish/rest/services/OpenData/ADSDI_OpenData/MapServer/828): 374 polygon-layer feature attribute rows under 372 distinct source project IDs and 371 distinct names, retrieved 2 November 2026. Two independent ordered API reads matched the exact 65,413 decoded bytes (SHA-256 `68bb6355c7847fba5f32bbe38f965b883f2e02ff60a56517d54d67c3ef5caa94`). The checksum-verified immutable snapshot is available through the read-only, source-labelled `/map/api/uae-evidence/abu-dhabi/urban-projects` API with search, pagination and feature detail. The source is in the publisher's OpenData service; the [AD-SDI catalog terms](https://sdi.gov.abudhabi/sdi/opendata.html) permit open-data use and redistribution with catalog credit. We excluded the `PROJECTLEAD` field and polygon geometry. This layer includes housing, hotels, ports and other urban developments; it is not a current real-estate project census, a registered-sales source, or 374 distinct projects. No V48 identity join, status, or map coordinate is inferred.
- [Dubai DLD Project Construction Database](https://continentalclub.ae/en/project-status/), republished by Continental Club Property under CC BY 4.0 from the DLD Mashrooi register: dated 1 November 2026, with 3,871 CSV rows and 3,871 distinct DLD project numbers. Two independent direct CSV downloads matched all 578,534 bytes (SHA-256 `3a1df52f5116bd8b14babe4cb44fa5fcdb31097e6c6e2fc366f6327f96a2be2f`). The republisher's page says 16 fields, but the actual downloadable CSV has 14; the backend validates and serves those 14 through `/map/api/uae-evidence/dubai/project-status`. Of 3,871 rows, 2,963 have an inspection date and 3,407 have a declared handover date. Completion percentages are last recorded inspection values, not live construction progress; handover dates are declarations, not actual delivery. The source has no geometry, transaction prices, or V48 identity crosswalk. It is a licensed secondary snapshot of the authority register, not a direct DLD API, an all-UAE census, or 3,871 new map pins.

2. Prioritize official project registers for Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain, Sharjah, and Ras Al Khaimah, then validate IDs, dates, status and coordinates before adding any pins. Current V48 project counts are especially thin in Fujairah and Ajman. The Ajman register is available now but cannot be converted into 298 pins without verified coordinates.
3. Resolve Abu Dhabi ADREC V55 reuse rights before publishing its separate registered-sale candidate. The user confirmed on 2 November that no approval is currently available, so its 642 sale observations remain unpublished. The 127 Abu Dhabi V48 records without verified coordinates remain searchable.

5. Restore a current PSR Data Hub source after D1 capacity recovers and reduce repeated row reads, especially the full project-filter scan repeated on every page. Reconcile all live pages, registry totals, and content hash before treating the D1 export as superseded. A paid capacity change requires a separate cost decision.
6. For each new source, capture publisher metadata and licence, preserve immutable source bytes and checksums, validate row grain and quality, publish a source-labelled API, stage at 0%, verify live readback and restrictions, then promote and recheck normal traffic.
