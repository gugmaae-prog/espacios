# Dubai 2026 Flat sale aggregates, partial year

The source is the licensed [Dubai Real Estate Data transaction copy](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dubairealestatedata/dubai-real-estate-sales-transactions) based on [Dubai Land Department open data](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/open-data/real-estate-data/). The source snapshot is dated 2 November 2026; the pinned Parquet SHA-256 is `8294c3dd83dde2e16e64eaf1ef155a1ad14ec16b6b39384561777afb8425417e`. The [publisher license](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dubairealestatedata/dubai-real-estate-sales-transactions/blob/main/LICENSE.md) is CC BY 4.0 with attribution. The [data dictionary](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dubairealestatedata/dubai-real-estate-sales-transactions/blob/main/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) defines registrations, Ready and Off-Plan, area IDs, price per square foot, and quality flags.

`scripts/build-dubai-flat-sales-2026.py` selects sale registrations from 1 January through 31 October 2026 where usage is `Residential`, property type is `Unit`, and subtype is exactly `Flat`. This excludes the Office/Shop rows whose source usage labels conflict after 9 February. The 105,473 selected transaction IDs are unique; 103,804 rows have `quality_flags = 0`. Ready has 26,973 registrations; Off-Plan has 78,500. They remain separate.

The output contains 20 month × registration groups and 1,117 DLD area × month × registration groups, spanning 70 known area IDs and 35 sales without an area ID. Counts include flagged source rows. Monetary sums and medians use only quality-zero rows. For 316 area-month groups with fewer than 10 clean records, both the monetary sum and price-per-square-foot median are `null`, meaning withheld rather than zero. Monthly totals are independently aggregated from source rows, not computed by averaging area medians. The API exposes named rows and source metadata at `/map/api/uae-evidence/dubai/flat-sales-2026-partial` with `view=monthly` or paginated `view=areas`.

These are registered flat sales, not asking listings, rent, villas, current available units, a valuation, forecast, or complete 2026. DLD cadastral area IDs are not unit coordinates. This source adds a bounded Dubai observation series to the seven-emirate inventory; it does not make the whole UAE complete. Run `python3 scripts/build-dubai-flat-sales-2026.py --check` against the committed gzip and validation report, then `node --test tests/dubai-flat-sales.test.mjs tests/uae-coverage.test.mjs` for the API and Worker assertions.
