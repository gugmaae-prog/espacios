# Dubai Ejari community rent aggregates — pinned 1 November 2026 snapshot

This source is the [Dubai Data / Continental Club Property aggregate dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/datadubai/dubai-real-estate-dld/tree/28fcfe459d3bf135a2e266dcdacdbe9575cf2ea7), based on Dubai Land Department Ejari open data and published under CC BY 4.0 with attribution. The pinned dataset commit is `28fcfe459d3bf135a2e266dcdacdbe9575cf2ea7` (repository updated 1 November 2026), and its README says the source data runs through 29 October 2026. The publisher calls the rows a trailing 12-complete-month snapshot. October 2025–September 2026 follows from that rule, but the CSVs do not carry explicit observation start/end fields. The [publisher methodology](https://datadubai.ae/methodology/) says the annual amounts come from registered, single-property Ejari contracts and include renewals. It is a publisher-derived aggregate, not a direct DLD CSV.

Two independent downloads of each pinned CSV matched. `rents_by_community.csv` is 8,896 bytes, SHA-256 `0d0ef0d66da68a45867b8219c0be1efb8b83299ef3e1658f0eb6e2f60cdff213`; `community_bedrooms.csv` is 66,719 bytes, SHA-256 `623a99aec05b8959ab698ec742d45d18e5b58851a7b96396a4ca13cc72423b79`. The committed raw CSVs are the immutable inputs. Run `python3 scripts/build-dubai-ejari-rents.py --check` to rebuild and compare the source-labelled JSON and validation report.

The read-only `/map/api/uae-evidence/dubai/ejari-community-rents` endpoint has two distinct views: 102 community rows with 304,464 reported contracts, and 418 community × bedroom rows with 286,628 reported contracts. The populations overlap, so their counts must not be added. The bedroom view has 66 null medians where fewer than five contracts were registered, including five Palm Jabal Ali bedroom rows with zero contracts. Another 33 medians have only 5–19 contracts and retain a low-sample label. Null means withheld or unavailable, not zero annual rent.

Only registered rent counts and annual medians were admitted. The source's sale medians, sale sample quality, and derived yield fields were excluded from this rent endpoint. Property class is not encoded in either CSV, and publisher bedroom labels are not proof of apartment-only contracts. The community median can mix home types and renewals; it is not a new-tenant asking rent, commercial-space rent, specific unit valuation, current availability, or forecast. No source row supplies a verified unit coordinate or a V48 identity crosswalk, so this release adds no map pin and does not overwrite native V48 rent series.

The Map's existing private IDrive E2 `MapEvidence` binding is read-only and allowlisted for the 2,717 accepted V48 recovery objects. This Ejari snapshot is stored in the source repository and compiled Worker for the v64 API; it has **not** been uploaded to IDrive. Replicating it to IDrive requires a scoped private publisher upload, allowlist update, and independent byte/checksum readback before switching the endpoint's primary source.
