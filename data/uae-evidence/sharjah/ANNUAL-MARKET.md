# Sharjah annual market aggregates

Selected Sharjah Real Estate Registration Department disclosures for full calendar years 2022–2025 appear in the [annual-market data](annual-market-20261109.json) and [validation receipt](annual-market-validation.json). Sharjah 24 reported the [2022](https://sharjah24.ae/ar/articles/2023/01/19/zx50), [2023](https://sharjah24.ae/ar/Articles/2024/01/31/al099), [2024](https://sharjah24.ae/en/Articles/2025/01/22/AED-40-billion-Sharjahs-real-estate-transactions-in-2024), and [2025](https://sharjah24.ae/en/Articles/2026/01/20/kmr1) year-end results. Emirates News Agency separately reported the selected [2022](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/hszrgaee-sharjah%E2%80%99s-real-estate-transactions-topped-aed24), [2023](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/b1fg7a2-165-surge-real-estate-investments-sharjah-during), [2024](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/bhtgrp8-sharjah%E2%80%99s-real-estate-transactions-hit-record), and [2025](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/bybncv4-sharjah-real-estate-hits-record-aed656-billion) figures.

The 2022 report gives approximately AED 24 billion in trading value, 91,507 overall registrations, and 7,267 sales. Its sale-use categories reconcile exactly: 5,373 residential, 807 commercial, 760 industrial, and 327 agricultural. These are historical reported sales, not available units. The 2023 report gives approximately AED 27.1 billion in trading value, 8,731 sales, and five newly registered projects; its 24,842 property-deed transactions are a narrower category and are **not** used as an overall registration count. The 2024 report gives approximately AED 40 billion in trading value and 14 newly registered projects; exact overall registration and sale counts remain null. The 2025 report gives approximately AED 65.6 billion in trading value, 132,659 overall registration transactions, and 33,580 sales; new-project count remains null. No count is back-solved from a percentage.

These full-year totals are separate from the existing 2026 H1 and January–September cumulative observations. Trading value includes transaction types beyond sales. Only 2022 has a reported commercial-use sale count; later annual reports here do not isolate it. The annual counts do not identify individual transactions, project records, or currently available spaces. Values are reported rounded totals; no price per unit or return is inferred.

Only selected numerical facts and source links are served by `/map/api/uae-evidence/sharjah/annual-market`. Article text, images, logos and full tables are excluded. The receipt records two independently retrieved HTML pass hashes for each article; dynamic page bytes differed while the selected facts agreed. No bulk transaction-register reuse right is asserted.
