# Sharjah annual market aggregates

The [annual-market data](annual-market-20261110.json) and [validation receipt](annual-market-validation.json) contain selected Sharjah Real Estate Registration Department year-end figures for **2018–2025**. The new 2018–2021 observations extend the previously published 2022–2025 series without changing its figures. This is a backend history of emirate-wide trading and registrations, not a register of individual transactions or an inventory of available units.

The 2018–2020 [official annual reports](https://shjrerd.gov.ae/reports/144) were retrieved and hashed. The 2018 report gives AED 22.5 billion trading value, 54,125 overall registrations and 3,254 sales; the 2019 report gives AED 24.2 billion, 61,357 and 3,328; the 2020 report gives AED 15.9 billion, 64,459 and 3,773. The [2018](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/hszr83sm-sharjah-realty-deals-stood-aed225-billion-2018) and [2019](https://sharjah24.ae/ar/Articles/2020/01/19/article-1643) disclosures, with independent [2019 WAM coverage](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/hszr9s5b-sharjah-real-estate-transactions-reached-aed242), and the [2020 department disclosure](https://sharjah24.ae/en/articles/2021/02/08/Sharjah-Real-Estate-transactions-value-record-AED-159-bn-in-2020) corroborate those selected facts. The 2018–2020 reports give commercial-use shares of 18.4%, 15.9% and 11.1% of reported sales. The exact commercial sale counts are **not** published in these selected disclosures and remain null.

The [2021 disclosure](https://sharjah24.ae/en/Articles/2022/02/09/Real-estate-trading-volume-in-the-Emirate-of-Sharjah-reaches-its-highest-level-in-4-years) gives AED 26.2 billion in trading value, 84,238 overall registrations, 6,304 sales and seven newly registered projects. A department archive entry labelled “Annual report 2021” actually returns a 2022 report. It is excluded as 2021 PDF proof. Its capture receipt is retained only to document that mismatch.

The existing [2022](https://sharjah24.ae/ar/articles/2023/01/19/zx50), [2023](https://sharjah24.ae/ar/Articles/2024/01/31/al099), [2024](https://sharjah24.ae/en/Articles/2025/01/22/AED-40-billion-Sharjahs-real-estate-transactions-in-2024) and [2025](https://sharjah24.ae/en/Articles/2026/01/20/kmr1) observations retain their earlier receipts. Only 2022 has an exact commercial-use sale count: 807 of 7,267 sales. 2023 and 2024 overall registration counts and 2024 sales remain null. Trading values include transaction types beyond sales. No count is back-solved from a share or percentage.

`/map/api/uae-evidence/sharjah/annual-market` serves selected numbers, source links and per-year filters. The commercial page reads the same backend series and labels it as historical context. Full report PDFs, article text, images and logos are not republished. The source does not provide a project register, project coordinates, available commercial spaces or transaction-level sale prices.
