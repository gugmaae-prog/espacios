# Fujairah building-permit figures

Sources: Fujairah Statistics Centre, Construction and Building chapters in the [2023 edition](https://www.fscfuj.gov.ae/WebsiteFiles/Reports/1045/Chapter2ConstructionandBuilding.pdf), [2024 edition](https://www.fscfuj.gov.ae/WebsiteFiles/Reports/1060/Chapter2ConstructionandBuilding.pdf) and [2025 edition](https://www.fscfuj.gov.ae/WebsiteFiles/Reports/1077/Chapter2ConstructionandBuilding.pdf), Tables 2-4 and 2-5, printed pages 50–51. The tables name Fujairah Municipality and Dibba Municipality as their underlying sources. The 2024 and 2025 PDFs were retrieved on 9 November 2026. The 2023 PDF was retrieved twice on 10 November 2026 and is 969,132 bytes with SHA-256 `7182e94f918f81e1c0b28795aac4d3890974efe836b4974b1facceffb35b6cb7`. The 2024 PDF is 5,100,936 bytes with SHA-256 `fdb36502f4956eb5de20d9d851e79372ef9cd7ce6730b7737b5ce5e0c091ff78`; the 2025 PDF is 1,317,448 bytes with SHA-256 `e8a4ed335e435a6c0460c0dcb395a85fab64113b2dc080aca9414bd1863f4721`.

The committed [selected facts](building-permits-20261110.json) contain annual issued-permit counts for 2021–2025 by municipality and permit type, plus three selected building-use categories for each of 2023, 2024 and 2025. The 2023 chapter adds the 2021 annual row and selected 2023 building uses. The newer chapters repeat 2023–2024 annual rows; they match the retained values. Each municipality's permit-type components reconcile to its printed total, and the municipal totals reconcile to the emirate total. The selected building-use categories reconcile separately across municipalities. See the [validation receipt](building-permits-validation.json) for data and source checksums.

The sources do not split new from renewed permits. Building-use types in Table 2-5 describe the same permits as Table 2-4 for their respective year; the two breakdowns cannot be added. Commercial-only, mixed residential-commercial, and industrial buildings remain separate categories. These are authorizations, not completed buildings, spaces for sale or rent, registered transactions, a project register, or geocoded properties.

Only selected numerical facts and source links are published. The PDFs and their images, prose, layout, and complete tables are not distributed through Espacios. No dataset-specific bulk redistribution right was established. The read-only API is `/map/api/uae-evidence/fujairah/building-permits`; the commercial page displays the 2025 commercial-only count as historical permit evidence, separate from RGEstate asking listings.
