# RGEstate factual snapshot refresh, 9 November 2026

The publisher's [full index](https://www.rgestate.com/properties) was captured
twice across 80 pages per pass. The [commercial rent](https://www.rgestate.com/property-for-rent/commercial)
and [commercial sale](https://www.rgestate.com/property-for-sale/commercial)
categories were separately captured twice across 22 and 20 pages per pass.
Every page has a byte count and SHA-256 receipt in the validation reports. The
two parsed passes agree exactly, and each commercial row matches the full index.
No listing photos, phone numbers, or advertiser contacts are retained.

| Source | 3 November snapshot | 9 November snapshot | Reviewed area references |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Full publisher index | 1,708 | 1,670 | 837 of the 1,067 rows without an explicit emirate label |
| Commercial rent category | 477 | 452 | 433 |
| Commercial sale category | 417 | 411 | 375 |
| Both commercial categories | 894 | 863 | 808; 55 remain list-only |

The area review added 20 exact publisher-label matches to named
[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) city or district
references. The exact OSM object links, approximate centres, and accepted
aliases are in `RGE_AREA_CENTRES`; the SHA-256 of that reference table is in
`area-geography-validation.json`. These matches add 105 rows to the full-index
area classification, including two in Abu Dhabi, and allow 22 more commercial
listings to appear in area circles. They are **area references, not unit
coordinates**. Ambiguous
labels such as Al Layan, Al Nahda, Dubai World Central, and Sheikh Zayed Road
remain list-only. A location reference does not confirm current availability.

The newer full index has 38 fewer visible IDs, no new IDs, and four changed
asking-price labels on continuing IDs. The newer commercial categories have 31
fewer visible IDs, no new IDs, and three changed asking-price labels on
continuing IDs. The exact IDs and old/new labels are in the `comparison` field
of each validation report and the read-only APIs. **An absent ID does not prove
a sale or withdrawal.** Asking prices are advertiser claims, not registered
transactions or valuation evidence. The snapshot cannot establish current
availability, an exact building location, or complete UAE inventory.

The 3 November compressed snapshots and their validation reports remain in this
directory. `work/rgestate-capture/` contains local raw HTML receipts for review
and is excluded from the release. The public Worker embeds only the verified,
photo-free derived facts and reports.
