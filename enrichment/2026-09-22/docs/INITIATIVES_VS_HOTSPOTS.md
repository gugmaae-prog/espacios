# Initiatives enrichment vs Emerging Hotspots

As of 2026-09-22 these are **aligned, not duplicated**:

| Layer | What it is | UI surface |
|---|---|---|
| **Initiatives enrichment** | Per-initiative catalyst intelligence (deliveryCertainty, linked catalogue project counts by area-name match, catalystScore 0–100, research notes) | Initiative detail panel (Future plan pins) |
| **Emerging Hotspots** (prior prep) | Community/area band scores (emerging_hotspot / watchlist / monitor) from pipeline + catalyst tags | Analyze / layer toggle — do **not** re-label as a second "initiatives" panel |

Naming rule: keep **Future initiatives** for pins; keep **Emerging hotspots** for the area band overlay. Catalyst language in the initiative panel should say **Catalyst score**, not “hotspot score”.
